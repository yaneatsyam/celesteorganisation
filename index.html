★ WHAT IS CELESTE?

Celeste Organisation is an independent creative and literary space built around young voices, experimentation, imagination and the strange little things that make being alive feel worth documenting.

We make room for poetry, prose, art, criticism, culture, conversations and everything that refuses to fit neatly into one category.
